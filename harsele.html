Härsele

A portal to rural Sweden

The Mysterious Rural Sweden

Region Västerbotten is a beautiful and rural part of Northern Sweden. Yet, population are sparse and these areas are often forgotten. How can we generate curiosity for rural Sweden among people living in the cities?

Härsele aims to provide visibility and foster outsider's interest, by allowing the voices of the rural areas to be heard, and its candid culture and wonderful hospitality to be seen.
